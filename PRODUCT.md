# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, technical practitioners, and curious readers use Stephanie Jarmak's site to move between papers, concepts, digests, talks, and authored work. The library should support both deliberate study and quick evidence retrieval without assuming that every visitor already knows the field's vocabulary.

## Product Purpose

The site is a public research notebook and portfolio. Its library turns curated scientific sources into navigable collections, thematic literature maps, and reading paths while keeping the original sources close enough to verify every claim.

## Brand Personality

Warm, rigorous, and curious. The interface should feel like an archival atlas maintained by a working information scientist: edited rather than ornamental, technically confident without becoming clinical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, glossy AI marketing pages, glass effects, decorative metric tiles, identical card grids, and interfaces that bury source provenance. Do not imitate the visual identity of third-party collections when integrating their material.

## Design Principles

1. Put the source beside the synthesis so readers can verify what they see.
2. Make dense collections scannable without flattening their intellectual structure.
3. Let the existing warm archival system carry new tools instead of inventing a separate visual language.
4. Use progressive disclosure for detail while keeping titles, authors, dates, and routes visible.
5. Treat mobile, keyboard, reduced-motion, and color-independent states as part of the primary experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast in both themes, 44-pixel touch targets for primary controls, reduced-motion behavior, and labels that do not depend on color alone.
