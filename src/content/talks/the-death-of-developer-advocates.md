description: Upcoming talk at the AI Engineer World's Fair in San Francisco — Thursday, July 2, 2026, 11:10am. As coding agents absorb the work developer advocates once did by hand — the docs, the demos, the sample apps — the role doesn't die so much as invert. Enter the Agent Advocate — someone who speaks for the agents, treating their failures in real codebases as retrieval and tooling problems rather than proof the model isn't smart enough. Drawing on agent-native work at Sourcegraph, from measuring how AI tools discover and describe products to turning messy production data into context agents can actually use, the talk reframes advocacy as building the infrastructure agents depend on — not just the content they replace.
