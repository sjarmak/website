This one's by hand, not by cron. These are the pieces I starred and came back to.

The throughline: the model is no longer the bottleneck most weeks — the **harness**
is. Retrieval quality, context budgeting, and how you structure verification matter
more than another point of raw capability. The toy-repo benchmark critique is the
sharp version of the same argument: capability measured on clean, small repos doesn't
survive contact with a real monorepo.
